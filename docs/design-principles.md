# Design Principles

## Typography

IBM Plex Mono is the primary typeface across the portfolio.

Individual case studies may introduce additional typefaces when they are part of the project's original visual identity.

---

## Color

The portfolio is built primarily around:

- near-black
- white
- grayscale tones

Magenta is used sparingly as an accent for emphasis, numbering, interaction and selected details.

Case studies may retain their own original color systems where appropriate.

---

## Navigation Motion

The navigation interaction is derived from the geometry of the menu icon.

Two independent rounded strokes animate upward to frame the text.

The strokes intentionally stop short of touching, leaving a small gap that reflects the broken-loop motif used throughout the identity.

The animation communicates framing rather than enclosure, reinforcing Alex's background in photography and visual composition.

---

## Image Treatment

Photography and project imagery generally begin in grayscale and reveal color through interaction where appropriate.

Images are treated as visual material rather than decorative backgrounds, with restrained scaling and movement used to create interaction without overwhelming the work.

---

## Layout

The portfolio uses a restrained editorial structure:

- large typography
- generous negative space
- asymmetric compositions
- strong visual hierarchy
- minimal decorative elements

The interface should support the work rather than compete with it.

---

## Motion

Animations should feel deliberate and restrained.

Movement is primarily used to:

- introduce content
- establish hierarchy
- provide feedback
- reveal relationships between elements

Animations should not exist purely for decoration.

---

## Case Study Identity

The portfolio has its own visual language, but individual projects are allowed to retain elements of their original identities.

This includes:

- typography
- color
- imagery
- graphic systems

The case study should feel like part of the portfolio while still representing the project authentically.
